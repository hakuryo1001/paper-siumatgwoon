the siumatgwoon investigation so far has been very algebraic and its approach and perspective. Not very “sentential” or “Aristotlean” at all. I am not extracting sentence structures and formalizing them like Aristotle or Panini. I am studying the mathematical object as if it is a group or a ring. Unlike traditional approaches to logic where I define a list of symbols and the rules for their composition such that the composed is a well formed formula such that it would have sense, thereby allowing us to simply focus on the evaluation question of whether it is true or false, the current naive composition rules allowed by the siumatgwoon rules allow for the naive and structureless production of symbol strings. there’s no connection between the structure of those strings and their sense. In propositional logic and predicate logic this is not a problem because there are rules as to what a well formed formula is and all well formed formula are sensible and can therefore be subjected to questions or whether it is true or false.  As such, there’s no real exercise of logic. Logic is about entailment, and entailment is about the preservation of truth because of structure. There is no entry point for an investigation of the preservation of truth here. 

Perhaps a naive and straightforward and even perhaps enticing and intuitive approach to building a sentence construction rule set of siumatgwoon is to perhaps simply treat all strings constructed from symbols taken from a siumatgwoon to be sub propositional or sub predicate structures. In other words the strings composed by siumatgwoon symbols are the atomistic propositions in propositional logic, the predicate and names in predicate logic. 

This is very attractive because it basically takes us to the old question of analyticity. Kantian analyticity. “The predicate is contained within the subject”. The kind of analyticity that Quine tried to kill. “All bachelors are unmarried” is a tautology. But you cannot see its tautologicality by naive predicate translation. You need to decompose the predicate “bachelor” and argue “unmarried” is “contained” within the concept of “bachelor”. This is where a siumatgwoon logic could live
